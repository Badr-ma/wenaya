# Understanding Chronic Inflammation

Acute inflammation is your body's natural defense mechanism. Chronic inflammation, however, is a silent driver of nearly every major disease of aging.

## The Inflammatory Cascade

When inflammation becomes chronic, it creates a self-perpetuating cycle of cellular damage and immune activation. This process is linked to:

- Cardiovascular disease
- Type 2 diabetes
- Neurodegenerative conditions
- Autoimmune disorders
- Accelerated aging

### Measuring Inflammation

Standard tests often miss low-grade chronic inflammation. Our comprehensive panel includes:

- High-sensitivity CRP
- Cytokine profiling (IL-6, TNF-alpha)
- Fibrinogen levels
- Homocysteine
- Oxidized LDL

> "Chronic inflammation is the fire that smolders beneath most modern diseases. Put out the fire, and you dramatically reduce your disease risk." — Dr. Sarah Mitchell

## Lifestyle Interventions That Work

Research shows these interventions effectively reduce inflammatory markers:

1. **Anti-inflammatory nutrition**: Omega-3 rich foods, polyphenols, fiber
2. **Regular physical activity**: Especially moderate-intensity exercise
3. **Stress management**: Meditation, sleep optimization
4. **Environmental toxins**: Reducing exposure to pollutants and endocrine disruptors

## Monitoring Progress

The key is measuring what matters. We track inflammatory markers over time to validate that your interventions are working, and adjust when they're not.
