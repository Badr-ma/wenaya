# The Science of Personalized Nutrition

For decades, dietary advice has taken a one-size-fits-all approach. But advances in nutritional biochemistry reveal that our individual responses to the same foods vary dramatically.

## Why Generic Advice Fails

The same diet can produce vastly different results in different people due to:

- Genetic variations in metabolism
- Gut microbiome composition
- Current biomarker status
- Lifestyle and environmental factors

### The Biochemistry of You

Personalized nutrition starts with understanding your unique biochemical landscape. By analyzing specific biomarkers, we can determine exactly which nutrients you need — and just as importantly, which you don't.

**Key markers we use**

- Amino acid profiles
- Fatty acid ratios (Omega-3 index)
- Vitamin and mineral status
- Inflammatory markers
- Hormonal balance indicators

> "There is no such thing as a universally 'healthy' diet. There's only what's healthy for you, right now, given your current biology." — Elena Voss

## Case Study: Omega-3 Supplementation

A generic recommendation might be "take 2g of fish oil daily." But your optimal dose depends on:

1. Your current Omega-3 index
2. Your inflammatory marker levels
3. Your genetic variants in FADS genes
4. Your dietary intake patterns
