# Understanding Your Biomarkers

Your blood contains an extraordinary amount of information about your health. But most standard tests only scratch the surface, analyzing 30-40 markers at most.

## Why 525+ Biomarkers Matter

Think of your health as a complex symphony. A 30-marker panel is like listening to just the violins. Our 525+ analysis gives you the full orchestra.

### Categories We Analyze

**Metabolic Health**

- Glucose regulation markers
- Lipid profiles (beyond basic cholesterol)
- Metabolic rate indicators

**Inflammatory Markers**

- High-sensitivity CRP
- Cytokine profiles
- Oxidative stress markers

**Hormonal Balance**

- Thyroid panel (complete)
- Adrenal stress markers
- Sex hormone optimization

**Nutritional Status**

- Vitamin and mineral levels
- Amino acid profiles
- Fatty acid ratios

**Organ Function**

- Liver enzymes (comprehensive)
- Kidney function markers
- Pancreatic health indicators

## The 525+ Advantage

Most standard panels miss critical early warning signals. For example, subtle changes in certain inflammatory markers can predict cardiovascular risk years before traditional markers show anything.
