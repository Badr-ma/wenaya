# The Foundation of Health

Sleep is not merely rest — it's an active physiological process essential for cellular repair, cognitive function, and hormonal balance.

## Why Sleep Matters

Poor sleep quality is linked to:

- Impaired glucose metabolism
- Increased cortisol and stress
- Reduced immune function
- Accelerated cognitive decline
- Disrupted hormone production

### Measuring Sleep Quality

We go beyond "how many hours" to analyze:

- Cortisol awakening response
- Melatonin metabolite levels
- Growth hormone markers
- Inflammatory cytokine patterns
- Glucose variability

> "Sleep is the single most effective intervention for improving biomarkers across every category." — Dr. Sarah Mitchell

## Circadian Optimization Protocol

Our evidence-based approach includes:

1. Light exposure management
2. Meal timing alignment
3. Temperature regulation
4. Stress reduction before bed
5. Supplement support when indicated

## Tracking Progress

We measure sleep quality objectively through biomarker analysis, not just subjective feeling. When your sleep improves, we can see it in your cortisol curve, glucose stability, and inflammatory markers.
